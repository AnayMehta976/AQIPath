# Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

AQIPath helps you find routes with cleaner air.

## What the app uses

- **Location.** Used to compute routes, generate loops and show local AQI. Location may be shared with third-party routing and air quality data providers to fulfil requests.
- **Personal information.** Limited information may be collected as described on the Google Play Data safety section.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit.

## Your choices

You can deny or revoke location permission in Android settings (some features will not work). To request deletion of your data, email anaymht12@gmail.com.
